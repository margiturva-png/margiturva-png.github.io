User experience for the Xolo mobile app

Native iOS and Android apps for freelancers running their business from anywhere.

🏆 Shortlisted at the Kuldmuna awards
Scroll
01
Introduction

Specialised mobile knowledge for an in‑house team

Xolo (formerly LeapIN) wanted to build native Android and iOS apps. They had hired a contract designer but needed specialised mobile development and design knowledge. I joined as the user experience designer, making sure the user flows, components and patterns suited the product.

Product

Xolo is a fintech app for freelancers that helps them run their business from anywhere, with secure banking, accounting, and one of the simplest tax systems in the world.

The first release covered invoicing, expense and income management, business trips and customer profiles.

02
Process

From user stories to a validated design

The scope of the first release was agreed between Xolo's product manager and the agency's project manager. I used the project manager's user stories to create the initial wireframes, which I reviewed with Xolo's product manager and UI designer. The UI designer provided the components and visual styles that we used for the first high‑fidelity design. We iterated on the designs through reviews with the product manager and UI designer. The final review was with the technical team, to validate the designs against the back end.

  1. User storiesProduct manager (client) · Project manager (agency)
  2. WireframesUX designer (me) · UI designer (contractor) · Product manager (client)
  3. High‑fidelity designUX designer (me) · UI designer (contractor) · Product manager (client) · Project manager (agency) · Technical team (client + agency)
03
Platform‑specific solutions

Android vs iOS user experience

The scope was fixed and the deadlines were set, so our solutions had to be precise and respect the time and resource limits. We balanced native and custom solutions to deliver a high return on investment.

As ideas went back and forth with the UI designer, I checked with development that solutions could be delivered on time and offered alternatives when needed.

04
Challenges & outcome

Communication as the key to launch

Working as a contractor for an in‑house product team was challenging. We lacked knowledge of the product, and the client team lacked knowledge of mobile development, so thorough communication was essential for a successful launch.

The app has solid adoption among its target group. Feedback on the product UX has been positive, and it was shortlisted in the e‑service category of Kuldmuna, the Estonian marketing and design awards.
